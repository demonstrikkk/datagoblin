Nova Labs closes £8M seed round, hiring across London

Nova Labs, a London machine-learning infrastructure startup founded by Amir Khan, has closed an £8M seed round. The round was led by Seedline Capital with participation from angels.

Founder Amir Khan said the funding will support hiring: Nova Labs currently advertises 7 open roles, five of them engineering positions in London. See https://nova-labs.example.com/careers.
